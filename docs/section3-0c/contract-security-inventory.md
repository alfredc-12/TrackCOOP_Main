## Section 4 ownership and compatibility

Section 4 owns `pos/**`, inventory/product and `rental/**` operational behavior, including source eligibility, checkout, stock, booking, pricing and release. Current inventory APIs authorize staff mutations; they are not already finance-read-only. Section 3 can build authorized reads/reconciliation and invoke established source-owned transitions; it must not invent a parallel writer. `pos_sales`, booking/payment IDs and payment-reference links are cross-team contract keys. Any proposed shared API/status/evidence or behavior change requires Section 4 developer review and source contract tests. Existing Bookkeeper confirmation and Chairman POS completion are distinct.
