[BLUE] NASA SYSTEM NA
============================================================

1. Clearly present the growth and transaction history of each member's Share Capital.
Details: The system has a Share Capital growth chart, current balance, initial capital, contributions, interest added, deductions, and posted transaction history.

2. Clarify the Annual Interest on Share Capital declaration, distribution schedule, and member options to withdraw or add interest to Share Capital.
Details: The system includes an annual declaration, coverage dates, distribution date, member withdrawal or Share Capital capitalization options, and Bookkeeper payout processing.

3. Implement a member referral system and record successful referrals for recruitment monitoring.
Details: The system provides active referral codes and shareable links, Copy/Share actions, live code validation, optional referral capture, duplicate-referral protection, pending-to-successful status tracking, referral progress display, in-app notifications, audit logging, member referral history, Chairman recruitment analytics with conversion rate, top referrers, barangay breakdowns, and an admin-configurable reward policy popup on Chairman > Members. Credits are tracking-only and do not create an automatic cash payout.

6. Define and clarify the purpose of seasonal analytics and how seasons affect production, sales, income, and expenses.
Details: Chairman Seasonal Analytics supports season configuration, Draft/Active/Closed status, date ranges, production-record entry, crop-level production totals, monthly sales/income/expense trends, financial-category breakdowns, net result, and margin. Existing POS and financial records without an explicit season tag are included when their dates fall inside the selected season range.

11. Provide a separate Expense Management module with a detailed breakdown of expenses.
Details: The system has Operating Expenses, expense categories, amount, payee, payment method, receipt, description, remarks, and financial ledger recording.

12. Implement budget controls or a safety net to prevent expenses from exceeding approved limits.
Details: Chairman Budget Controls supports a draft-to-approval workflow, category and season budget lines, warning percentages, approved limits, utilization tracking, unbudgeted-expense visibility, and an optional hard limit that blocks new expenses after the approved amount is exceeded. Operating Expenses can be assigned to an active season and receives a warning when a budget line approaches its limit.

13. Ensure financial transparency by allowing members to view appropriate income and expense breakdowns.
Details: The member dashboard shows posted cooperative income, expenses, net operating result, and category-level breakdowns. Share Capital contributions are identified separately. The Financial Transparency modal includes period filters, previous-period comparison, category details, definitions, reconciliation state, loading/empty/error states, fixed-bottom pagination, and Save as PDF.

15. Include product/shop analytics to identify most purchased products, sales performance, and demand.
Details: The Chairman Financial Dashboard includes live Product & Shop Analytics from POS and inventory data, including shop sales, units sold, transactions, average order value, sales by category, top purchased products, demand level, current stock, period filters, empty states, and links to POS and reports. Products & Inventory shows High, Medium, and Low demand tags, demand filtering/sorting, period-based demand calculations, and low-stock alerts.

16. Generate complete and detailed reports for relevant cooperative transactions and analytics.
Details: The system has financial, membership, Share Capital, rental, sales, inventory, document, audit, and notification reports.

18. Populate the system with sufficient realistic data to properly demonstrate analytics and reports.
Details: Testing data includes members, Share Capital, expenses, POS sales, products, inventory, rentals, reports, announcements, and notifications.


============================================================
[YELLOW] PARTIAL
============================================================

4. Include promotional features to support cooperative membership recruitment and activities.
Details: The system has announcements, public gallery content, landing-page content, and targeted announcements. A dedicated recruitment campaign and promotional analytics feature are not yet available.

7. Clearly define High-Value Crops and specify the criteria and crops included.
Details: High-Value Crops is available as a sector option, but the detailed criteria and complete crop list have not yet been defined in the system.

8. Clarify Barangay Recruitment Analytics, including membership and referral data per barangay.
Details: The system has member counts, barangay mapping, sector breakdowns, top barangays, and a Chairman referral summary with total, pending, successful, top-referrer, and referral-by-barangay data.

9. Confirm whether a person may belong to multiple sectors or whether a one-to-one relationship should be followed.
Details: The current system stores one sector per member. The cooperative still needs to officially confirm and document whether this one-to-one rule is correct.

10. Make members' earnings, income, and expenses visible and understandable in the system.
Details: Members can view statements, deposits, purchases, patronage, and Share Capital information. A complete personal income-and-expense breakdown is not yet available.

14. Use more detailed, realistic, and related data for dashboards and analytics during the Final Defense.
Details: The system has dashboards and seeded realistic data, but additional records and scenarios are still needed for a stronger Final Defense demonstration.

17. Implement pop-up, email, and SMS/text notifications for important system updates.
Details: In-app notifications, notification bells, announcements, and email support are available. Actual SMS/text provider integration is not yet implemented.

RECENT IMPLEMENTATION UPDATES
============================================================

- Added live product/shop analytics to the Chairman Financial Dashboard.
- Added demand-level tags, demand filters, demand sorting, and period-based demand calculations to Products & Inventory.
- Added responsive summary cards for products, high demand, stock value, cash sales, and QRPH sales.
- Replaced the Stock Alerts summary card with an inventory-opening stock-alert modal.
- Added direct Add Stock access from low-stock products and a detailed success toast after replenishment.

- Added member referral codes, shareable links, live validation, duplicate protection, referral history, recruitment analytics, and a Chairman-configurable reward policy.
- Added Seasonal Analytics with season configuration, season lifecycle status, production records, crop summaries, monthly performance, and financial category breakdowns.
- Added date-range fallback for existing POS and financial records so historical data appears in a selected season even before every record is explicitly tagged.
- Added Budget Controls with budget lines, approval workflow, warning thresholds, utilization, unbudgeted expense monitoring, and optional hard-limit blocking.
- Added an optional Season selector to Operating Expenses and budget warning feedback after a successful expense posting.
